Trump Announces 50% Tariff on Steel & Aluminum: Breaking News (May 31–June 2, 2025)

📌 Key Announcement

  • Tariff Increase: 50% on imported steel and aluminum (up from 25%).
  • Effective Date: June 4, 2025.
  • Platform: Announced via Truth Social.
  • Rationale: Protect U.S. industries and workers; Trump stated: “At 25%, [trading partners] can sorta get over that fence. At 50%, nobody’s getting over that fence.”

🌍 Global Reactions

  1. European Union Threatened countermeasures (Reuters). Called the move “economic aggression.”
  2. Markets
  • 📈 Steel stocks surged (e.g., Cleveland-Cliffs, U.S. Steel).
  • 📉 Auto stocks fell (Ford, GM) over cost concerns (WSJ).
  • Global indices dipped amid trade war fears (CNN).

Automakers Higher production costs
Consumers Price hikes for steel/aluminum products
Global Trade Retaliatory tariffs likely
